Privacy

Last updated October 2026

This page explains what the RAIN Lab website and its members dashboard keep about you. The lab is part of the William E. Boeing Department of Aeronautics & Astronautics at the University of Washington.

The public website

Anyone can read the public pages without signing in. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and fonts load from Google Fonts, so those services see the usual technical details your browser sends when it loads a page.

The members dashboard

The dashboard is only for current members of the lab. You sign in with your UW Google account, and only people on the lab's members list can get in. When you sign in, Google shares your name, your UW email address and your profile photo with us. We do not get your password or access to your email, files or calendar.

Inside the dashboard we keep what you choose to add, such as:

  • your profile details, and your birthday if you add it (month and day only)
  • talk sign-ups, reading groups, votes, review requests and papers you add to the lab library
  • news, profile or page changes you send for the public website, and requests to the lab admins

Who can see it

Only signed-in lab members can see dashboard content. Lab admins (Prof. Mesbahi and the lab's website admin) can also manage the members list and approve changes to the public website. Anything approved for the public website becomes public. We never sell or share your information.

Where it is stored

Dashboard data is stored with Supabase in the United States. The lab calendar stays in Google Calendar.

Emails

We only email you about the lab, for example when someone asks you to review a draft. You can turn emails off in the dashboard.

Removing your information

When you leave the lab, your access is removed. To have your dashboard information deleted, or for any question about this page, email Shiva Shakeri at sshakeri@uw.edu.
